Owner clarification: sub-sections are the writer's topic menu, inside the existing categories

The owner clarified on 2026-11-02 how the sub-sections of the brief (Photography Tips … Collector Interviews, Price Index items and the like) are meant:

- They are **usman's menu of article kinds**: a list of angles the writer picks from, so the Journal gets a wider and more directed range of articles, and articles of the same kind come back regularly as a series.
- Each kind **belongs to one existing category** (and, where it fits, an existing Topic). Example: Photography Tips → `guides-tutorials`; Collector Interviews → `retro-stories`; a Price Index item → `price-index`. Please fix this mapping in A's content plan for every kind in the brief.
- They are **not** created as categories, child categories, tags or menu items. The site structure stays as it is; the article kind shows in the title and text only (e.g. a short kicker or a fixed title pattern per kind, decided on A's side).

Also done today: the demo categories `classic`, `old-school`, `retro`, `vintage` were deleted on staging by the owner. Agents only see the existing Journal categories and Topics.
